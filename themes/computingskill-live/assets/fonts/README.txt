Self-hosted web fonts for the Phase 8 design (no third-party font requests).
- Chakra Petch 600/700 (Cadson Demak), latin subset, SIL Open Font License 1.1
- JetBrains Mono variable 400-700 (JetBrains), latin subset, SIL Open Font License 1.1
Files are the latin woff2 subsets served by Google Fonts. OFL text: https://openfontlicense.org

og/: TrueType copies of Chakra Petch 700 and JetBrains Mono (instanced at weight 500), converted from the woff2 files above with fontTools, same licence. Only used server-side by GD to draw the social share cards (inc/technical-seo.php); never sent to browsers.
